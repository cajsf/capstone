Accuracy/MAE:
• Gaze: 4-5° MAE (attention-based methods)
• Head Pose: 3-5° MAE (6DRepNet)
• Action Recognition: 99.75% (CNN-BiLSTM on distracted driving)
• Overall System: 95%+ achievable (your target)

Latency:
• Total budget: 0.3s = 300ms
• Realistic: 140-200ms (60% utilization)
• Components: 10-20ms (preprocess) + 20-40ms (face) + 50-80ms (gaze) + 30-50ms (pose)

Energy/Performance:
• EfficientNet: 20mJ, 40-60ms
• MobileNet: 25mJ, 50-80ms
• Distilled models: 15-18mJ, 30-50ms

Confidence Range:
• High: 0.7-1.0 (trust output)
• Medium: 0.4-0.7 (use but flag uncertainty)
• Low: <0.4 (use fallback or degraded mode)

Occlusion Handling:
• Single branch failure: confidence drops, other branches weight up
• Multi-branch failure: degraded mode, alert on uncertainty
• Full failure: request recalibration (confidence < threshold)
